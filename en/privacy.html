Data Transparency

Privacy Policy & Data Notice

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

1 Local Task Data & Device Storage

ArteMinder is built strictly on an offline-first architectural model. Any tasks, subtasks, dates, category tags, notification preferences, and application settings you configure are saved solely in local application storage on your device.

Artev Yazılım does not operate tracking servers or cloud sync databases that ingest or replicate your task descriptions. Your daily agenda remains entirely on your machine.

2 Network Access & Web Telemetry

During regular operation, ArteMinder requires no persistent internet connection. The application does not embed third-party advertising tracking SDKs, marketing trackers, or behavior surveillance frameworks.

The website (artevyazilim.com) serves static assets with industry-standard caching and does not install tracking advertising cookies on your browser.

3 Microsoft Store Operations

When downloading or updating ArteMinder through the Microsoft Store, installation and Store licensing transactions are handled directly by Microsoft infrastructure in accordance with the official Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Artev Yazılım receives only aggregate, non-identifying telemetry reports (such as cumulative download counts and crash crash-rate percentages) provided anonymously by Microsoft Partner Center.

4 Direct Support Communications

If you contact us voluntarily via email (e.g. at info@artevyazilim.com) to report an issue or ask a question, we use your email address and message contents solely to address and resolve your inquiry.

We do not sell, rent, or distribute support contacts to third-party marketing firms.
